## What makes a job cheaper or dearer

- **Number of windows.** Several windows in one visit cost less per window than a single call-out.
- **Matching old hardware.** Out-of-production stays, handles and locks may need to be sourced or substituted, which adds time. We carry common profiles and work with national suppliers to match most older joinery.
- **Access.** Upper-storey or awkward-access windows take longer than ground-floor ones.
- **Coastal corrosion.** Salt-affected hardware can be seized in place and slower to remove cleanly.

## Repair vs replace

A new aluminium window means joinery, glazing and installation, and often scaffolding. A repair restores the same window by fixing the part that actually failed: the stay, hinge, handle, lock or seal. Unless the frame or glass itself is damaged, a repair is almost always the cheaper and faster option.

## How to keep costs down

- Don't force a stiff window. It bends stays and strains hinges, turning a small fix into a bigger one.
- Deal with draughts early; perished seals let in water that corrodes hardware over time.
- Send a photo with your enquiry so we can bring the right parts on the first visit.
